Pakistani Nan Khatai Recipe

Nan Khatai is a popular South Asian-style buttery cookie known for its delicate, crumbly texture and lightly sweet flavor. These simple cookies are perfect with tea or coffee and make a lovely homemade treat.

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 15–20 minutes
Servings: About 12–15 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/2 cup ghee
  • 1/4 teaspoon cardamom powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons semolina
  • Chopped pistachios or almonds for topping

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 170°C (340°F).
  2. In a bowl, mix ghee and powdered sugar until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add flour, semolina, baking powder and cardamom powder.
  4. Mix gently until a soft dough forms. Avoid over-kneading.
  5. Divide the dough into small portions and shape them into round cookies.
  6. Place the cookies on a lined baking tray.
  7. Press a little chopped pistachio or almond on top of each cookie.
  8. Bake for about 15–20 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden.
  9. Allow the cookies to cool completely before serving.

Helpful Baking Tips

  • Use room-temperature ghee for a smoother dough.
  • Do not overmix the dough, as this can make the cookies harder.
  • Keep some space between the cookies on the baking tray.
  • Let the Nan Khatai cool before moving them because they are delicate when hot.
  • Store cooled cookies in an airtight container.

How to Serve Nan Khatai

Serve Nan Khatai with Pakistani chai, milk tea or coffee. They are also a delightful homemade treat for guests, family gatherings and festive occasions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nan Khatai?

Nan Khatai is a traditional-style South Asian shortbread cookie made with ingredients such as flour, sugar, ghee and cardamom.

Can I use butter instead of ghee?

Yes. Butter can be used as an alternative, although ghee gives the cookies their characteristic rich flavor and crumbly texture.

How should Nan Khatai be stored?

Once completely cooled, store the cookies in an airtight container at room temperature.
