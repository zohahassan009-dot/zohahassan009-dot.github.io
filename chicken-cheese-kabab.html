Chicken Cheese Kabab Recipe

Learn how to make crispy, golden chicken cheese kababs with a delicious melted cheese filling. These Pakistani-style kababs are perfect for family snacks, parties and Ramadan iftar.

Prep Time: 20 minutes

Cook Time: 20 minutes

Total Time: 40 minutes

Servings: 12 kababs

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked shredded chicken
  • 1 cup grated mozzarella cheese
  • 2 medium boiled potatoes, mashed
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons fresh coriander
  • 1 teaspoon ginger garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili flakes
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 beaten egg
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • Oil for shallow frying

How to Make Chicken Cheese Kabab

Step 1: Prepare the Mixture

In a large bowl, combine shredded chicken, mashed potatoes, chopped onion, green chilies, coriander, ginger garlic paste and all the spices. Mix everything thoroughly.

Step 2: Add the Cheese

Take a small portion of the mixture and flatten it in your palm. Place some mozzarella cheese in the center and carefully seal the edges. Shape into a round or oval kabab.

Step 3: Coat the Kababs

Dip each kabab into beaten egg and coat evenly with breadcrumbs. Repeat until all kababs are ready.

Step 4: Fry Until Golden

Heat oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Shallow fry the kababs for approximately 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown, crispy and heated through.

Step 5: Serve

Serve hot with mint chutney, ketchup or garlic mayonnaise. Enjoy the crispy outside and melted cheese inside!

Cooking Tips

  • Use fully cooked chicken for this recipe.
  • Chill the shaped kababs for 15 minutes if the mixture feels too soft.
  • Seal the cheese filling carefully to prevent leaking.
  • Fry over medium heat for an evenly golden crust.
