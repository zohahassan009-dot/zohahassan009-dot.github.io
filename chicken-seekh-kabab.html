Chicken Seekh Kabab

Learn how to make juicy and flavorful Pakistani-style chicken seekh kabab with minced chicken, fresh herbs and aromatic spices. This easy homemade recipe is perfect for dinner, family gatherings and special occasions.

Prep: 20 minutes
Cook: 15 minutes
Total: 35 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced chicken
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped and squeezed dry
  • 2 tablespoons fresh coriander, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon fresh mint, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons bread crumbs
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

How to Make Chicken Seekh Kabab

  1. Add minced chicken, squeezed onion, coriander, mint, green chili and ginger garlic paste to a large bowl.
  2. Add cumin powder, coriander powder, red chili powder, garam masala, black pepper, salt, lemon juice and bread crumbs.
  3. Mix everything thoroughly until the mixture is well combined.
  4. Cover and refrigerate the mixture for about 20–30 minutes. This helps make shaping easier.
  5. With slightly oiled hands, shape the mixture around seekh skewers or form long kababs by hand.
  6. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium heat and lightly brush it with oil.
  7. Place the kababs on the pan and cook, turning gently, until they are browned on the outside and cooked completely through.
  8. Serve hot with mint chutney, salad, lemon wedges or naan.

Cooking Tips

Tip: Make sure the chopped onion is squeezed very well before adding it to the chicken mixture. Too much moisture can make the kababs difficult to shape and may cause them to break while cooking.

Serving Suggestions

Chicken seekh kabab tastes delicious with mint chutney, fresh salad, lemon wedges, naan or homemade raita. It can also be served as part of a Pakistani-style dinner platter.
