Chicken Tikka

Chicken Tikka is a popular Pakistani dish made with tender chicken, yogurt, lemon and aromatic spices. Grilled until beautifully charred, it is perfect with naan, mint chutney and fresh salad.

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken pieces
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 tbsp tikka masala

Instructions

  1. Wash and pat dry the chicken. Make small cuts in the pieces so the marinade can absorb properly.
  2. In a bowl, mix yogurt, ginger garlic paste, lemon juice, red chili powder, paprika, cumin, coriander, garam masala, black pepper, salt, tikka masala and oil.
  3. Add the chicken and coat each piece thoroughly with the marinade.
  4. Cover and marinate for at least 2 hours. For the best flavor, refrigerate overnight.
  5. Preheat a grill, oven or pan. Cook the chicken over medium heat, turning occasionally, until completely cooked and nicely charred.
  6. Serve hot with lemon wedges, sliced onions, mint chutney and naan.
Cooking Tip:
For juicy and flavorful Chicken Tikka, marinate the chicken for several hours and avoid cooking it over very high heat for too long.

Serving Suggestions

Serve Chicken Tikka with naan, mint chutney, fresh salad, sliced onions and lemon wedges. It also makes a delicious addition to a Pakistani-style dinner table.
