Authentic Pakistani Mutton Karahi

Mutton Karahi is a rich and flavorful Pakistani dish made with tender mutton, fresh tomatoes, ginger, garlic and aromatic spices. This traditional-style karahi is perfect for a family dinner and tastes delicious with naan, roti or fresh tandoori bread.

Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 60 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 kg mutton, cut into medium pieces
  • 5 medium tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 5 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon crushed coriander seeds
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 3–4 green chilies, sliced
  • Fresh ginger, cut into thin strips
  • Fresh coriander, chopped

How to Make Mutton Karahi

  1. Heat oil or ghee in a large karahi or heavy-bottomed pan over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the mutton pieces and cook for several minutes until the meat changes color and begins to brown lightly.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for about one minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes, red chili powder, crushed coriander seeds, cumin powder, turmeric and salt. Stir everything together.
  5. Add about 1 cup of water, cover the pan and cook over medium-low heat until the mutton becomes tender. This may take around 45–60 minutes depending on the meat.
  6. Once the mutton is tender, remove the lid and cook for a few more minutes until the tomato mixture becomes thick and the oil begins to separate.
  7. Add freshly ground black pepper and green chilies. Cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  8. Garnish with fresh ginger and chopped coriander before serving.

Tips for a Flavorful Mutton Karahi

  • Use fresh and ripe tomatoes for a rich natural flavor.
  • Bone-in mutton can add extra flavor to the karahi.
  • Cook the mutton slowly until it becomes tender and juicy.
  • Do not add too much water because Mutton Karahi is best with a thick tomato base.
  • Cook the tomatoes well until the oil starts separating from the mixture.
  • Fresh ginger, coriander and green chilies give the dish a delicious finishing touch.

What to Serve With Mutton Karahi

Mutton Karahi is traditionally served hot with naan, roti or tandoori bread. A fresh salad, lemon wedges and chilled yogurt also make delicious accompaniments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use boneless mutton?

Yes. Boneless mutton can be used, although bone-in mutton often provides deeper flavor and a richer texture.

How long does Mutton Karahi take to cook?

Mutton usually takes around 45–60 minutes to become tender, depending on the cut and cooking method.

Can I make Mutton Karahi less spicy?

Yes. Reduce the amount of red chili powder and green chilies according to your preferred level of spice.

How do I know when Mutton Karahi is ready?

The mutton should be tender and fully cooked, while the tomato gravy should become thick and the oil should begin to separate from the mixture.
