Authentic Pakistani Chicken Biryani

Chicken Biryani is a beloved Pakistani rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, tender chicken, tomatoes, yogurt and aromatic spices. This flavorful layered meal is perfect for family dinners, gatherings and special occasions.

Prep Time: 25 minutes
Cook Time: 50 minutes
Servings: 5–6

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into medium pieces
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 teaspoons red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 cloves
  • 4 green cardamom pods
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander, chopped
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • 2–3 green chilies
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • A pinch of saffron soaked in warm milk, optional

How to Make Chicken Biryani

  1. Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for about 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a large pot and fry the sliced onions until golden. Remove some fried onions for garnishing.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for about one minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the chicken pieces and cook until they change color and begin to brown lightly.
  5. Add tomatoes, yogurt, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, cumin, salt and the whole spices. Mix well.
  6. Cover and cook over medium heat until the chicken becomes tender and the masala thickens.
  7. Meanwhile, boil plenty of salted water in a separate pot. Add the soaked rice and cook until the grains are partially cooked but still firm in the center.
  8. Drain the rice well.
  9. Layer half of the rice over the chicken masala. Add some mint, coriander, green chilies and fried onions.
  10. Add the remaining rice and finish with the remaining herbs, fried onions and optional saffron milk.
  11. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat for about 15–20 minutes. This steaming process helps the flavors come together.
  12. Gently fluff the biryani from the sides without breaking the rice. Serve hot.

Tips for Perfect Biryani

  • Use good-quality long-grain basmati rice.
  • Do not overcook the rice before layering.
  • Let the biryani steam on low heat so the rice stays fluffy.
  • Golden fried onions add extra flavor and aroma.
  • Adjust the chilies and spices according to your taste.

What to Serve With Chicken Biryani

Chicken Biryani pairs beautifully with raita, fresh salad, mint chutney and lemon wedges. A simple yogurt raita can balance the spices and make the meal even more enjoyable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use boneless chicken for Biryani?

Yes. Boneless chicken can be used, although bone-in chicken generally adds more flavor to the masala.

Why is my Biryani rice mushy?

Rice can become mushy when it is overcooked before layering or when too much liquid is used. Cook the rice only until it is partially done before assembling the biryani.

Can I make Biryani less spicy?

Yes. Reduce the amount of red chili powder and green chilies to suit your preferred spice level.
