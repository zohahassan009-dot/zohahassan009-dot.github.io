Authentic Pakistani Chicken Karahi

Chicken Karahi is one of Pakistan's most loved traditional dishes. It is made with tender chicken, fresh tomatoes, ginger, garlic and aromatic spices. This flavorful dish is perfect for a family dinner and tastes delicious with naan or roti.

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 35–40 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into medium pieces
  • 5 medium tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 4 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon crushed coriander seeds
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 3–4 green chilies, sliced
  • Fresh ginger, cut into thin strips
  • Fresh coriander, chopped

How to Make Chicken Karahi

  1. Heat oil or ghee in a large karahi or heavy-bottomed pan over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the chicken pieces and cook for several minutes until the chicken changes color and begins to brown lightly.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for about one minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes, red chili powder, coriander seeds, cumin, turmeric and salt. Stir everything together.
  5. Cover and cook over medium heat for about 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes become soft and the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Remove the lid and cook for a few more minutes until the tomato mixture becomes thick and the oil begins to separate.
  7. Add black pepper and green chilies. Cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  8. Garnish with fresh ginger and coriander before serving.

Tips for a Flavorful Chicken Karahi

  • Use ripe tomatoes for a naturally rich and flavorful sauce.
  • Fresh ginger and green chilies add an authentic finishing touch.
  • Do not add too much water; Karahi is best with a thick tomato base.
  • Cook the tomatoes well until the oil starts separating.
  • Serve hot with naan, roti or fresh tandoori bread.

What to Serve With Chicken Karahi

Chicken Karahi is traditionally enjoyed with naan or roti. A simple salad, lemon wedges and chilled yogurt can also make a delicious accompaniment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use boneless chicken?

Yes. Boneless chicken can be used, although bone-in chicken often provides deeper flavor.

Can I make Chicken Karahi less spicy?

Yes. Reduce the amount of red chili powder and green chilies according to your preferred level of spice.

How do I know when Chicken Karahi is ready?

The chicken should be fully cooked and tender, while the tomato gravy should become thick and the oil should begin to separate from the mixture.
