Red Velvet Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
  • 1 tablespoon red food coloring
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix flour, cocoa powder and baking soda in a bowl.
  3. In another bowl, whisk eggs, sugar, oil, buttermilk and vanilla essence.
  4. Add the red food coloring and mix well.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients and mix until smooth.
  6. Add vinegar and gently mix the batter.
  7. Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
  8. Bake for about 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  9. Allow the cake to cool completely before frosting.

Cream Cheese Frosting

Beat cream cheese with butter and powdered sugar until smooth and creamy. Spread the frosting over the completely cooled cake.

Cooking Tips

Do not overmix the batter. Let the cake cool completely before adding frosting for the best texture.
