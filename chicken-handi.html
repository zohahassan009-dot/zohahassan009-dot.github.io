Chicken Handi

Chicken Handi is a delicious Pakistani-style chicken dish made with tender chicken, tomatoes, yogurt, cream and aromatic spices. It is rich, flavorful and perfect for a family dinner.

Prep Time: 15 minutes Cook Time: 35 minutes Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1/2 cup cooking cream
  • 2 green chilies, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, julienned

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a handi or heavy pan. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  2. Add onion and cook until soft. Add ginger garlic paste and cook for one minute.
  3. Add tomatoes, cumin, coriander powder, red chili powder, turmeric and salt. Cook until the tomatoes soften and the oil separates.
  4. Lower the heat and gradually add yogurt while stirring. Cook until smooth.
  5. Cover and cook on medium-low heat until the chicken is tender and fully cooked.
  6. Add cream and garam masala. Mix gently and cook for another 3–5 minutes.
  7. Garnish with green chilies, fresh coriander and ginger.

Cooking Tips

  • Cook the masala well before adding the cream for better flavor.
  • Add yogurt on low heat to prevent it from curdling.
  • Do not overcook the chicken after adding cream.

Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with naan, roti, chapati or fragrant basmati rice. Fresh salad and raita also pair beautifully with Chicken Handi.
