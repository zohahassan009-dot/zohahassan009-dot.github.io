Classic Pakistani Chicken Korma

Chicken Korma is a comforting Pakistani and South Asian dish made with tender chicken, yogurt, aromatic spices and a rich, flavorful gravy. It is a wonderful choice for family dinners and special occasions.

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 45 minutes
Servings: 4–5

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into medium pieces
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 4 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3–4 cloves
  • 3 green cardamom pods
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1/2 cup water, or as needed
  • Fresh coriander for garnish
  • Fresh ginger, cut into thin strips

How to Make Chicken Korma

  1. Heat the oil or ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot. Add the sliced onions and cook until golden brown.
  2. Remove some of the fried onions for garnishing. Let them cool and crush them lightly.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste to the pot and cook for about one minute.
  4. Add the chicken pieces and cook over medium-high heat until the chicken changes color and develops a light golden surface.
  5. Add red chili powder, turmeric, coriander, cumin and salt. Stir well so the spices coat the chicken.
  6. Lower the heat. Whisk the yogurt until smooth and add it gradually while stirring continuously.
  7. Add the cinnamon, cloves, cardamom and bay leaf. Cook until the yogurt mixture becomes rich and the oil begins to separate.
  8. Add a little water, cover the pot and simmer for about 20–25 minutes, or until the chicken is completely tender.
  9. Add the crushed fried onions and garam masala. Cook for another 5 minutes so the flavors combine.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander and thin strips of ginger. Serve hot.

Tips for a Rich Korma

  • Cook the onions until properly golden for deeper flavor.
  • Add yogurt gradually and keep the heat low to help prevent curdling.
  • Allow the masala to cook until the oil begins to separate.
  • Use bone-in chicken for a richer and more flavorful gravy.
  • Adjust the spices according to your preferred heat level.

What to Serve With Chicken Korma

Chicken Korma is delicious with naan, roti, paratha or fragrant basmati rice. A fresh salad or simple yogurt raita also makes a refreshing side.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use boneless chicken?

Yes. Boneless chicken works well, although bone-in chicken can provide a richer flavor.

How can I make the Korma less spicy?

Reduce the amount of red chili powder and adjust the seasoning to your preferred taste.

Why does yogurt sometimes curdle?

Yogurt can curdle when added over very high heat. Whisk it until smooth, lower the heat and add it gradually while stirring.
