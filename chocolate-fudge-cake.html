Chocolate Fudge Cake

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
  • ½ cup hot water

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder and baking soda.
  3. Add eggs, milk, oil and vanilla essence. Mix until smooth.
  4. Gradually add hot water and mix well.
  5. Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
  6. Bake for about 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  7. Allow the cake to cool before adding chocolate frosting.
  8. Slice and serve.

Chocolate Frosting

Mix melted chocolate with cream until smooth, then spread over the cooled cake.

Cooking Tips

Do not overmix the batter. Let the cake cool completely before frosting for the best texture.
