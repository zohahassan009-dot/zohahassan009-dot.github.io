Chicken Haleem

Authentic Pakistani-style Chicken Haleem

About Chicken Haleem

Chicken Haleem is a delicious and comforting Pakistani dish made with tender chicken, wheat, lentils, barley and aromatic spices. Slow cooking gives it a rich, thick and creamy texture that makes it perfect for family meals and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken
  • 1/2 cup wheat
  • 1/4 cup chana dal
  • 1/4 cup masoor dal
  • 1/4 cup moong dal
  • 1/4 cup barley
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons ginger garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil

For Garnishing

  • Fresh coriander
  • Fried onions
  • Lemon wedges

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak the wheat, barley and lentils for several hours or overnight.
  2. Cook the soaked wheat, barley and lentils with water until they become very soft.
  3. Heat oil in a pot and sauté the sliced onion until golden. Add ginger garlic paste and cook for another minute.
  4. Add chicken, red chili powder, turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala and salt. Cook until the chicken becomes tender.
  5. Remove the chicken and shred it into small pieces.
  6. Combine the shredded chicken with the cooked grains and lentils.
  7. Blend or mash the mixture until it becomes smooth, thick and creamy.
  8. Cook the haleem on low heat for another 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander and fried onions and serve hot with lemon wedges.
Cooking Tip: For the best flavor and texture, cook Chicken Haleem slowly and stir it regularly while it thickens.

Serving Suggestions

Serve Chicken Haleem hot with fried onions, fresh coriander, green chilies and lemon wedges. It can also be enjoyed with naan or roti.
