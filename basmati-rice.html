Perfect Basmati Rice

Fluffy basmati rice is an essential part of many Pakistani meals. With the right amount of water, proper soaking and gentle cooking, you can make fragrant rice with beautifully separate grains at home.

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Soaking Time: 20–30 minutes
Cook Time: 15–20 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil or ghee
  • 1 bay leaf, optional
  • 2 green cardamom pods, optional
  • 1 small cinnamon stick, optional

How to Cook Basmati Rice

  1. Place the rice in a bowl and rinse it several times with cool water until the water becomes mostly clear.
  2. Soak the rice in clean water for 20–30 minutes. Drain it thoroughly before cooking.
  3. Add water to a large pot along with salt, oil or ghee and the optional whole spices.
  4. Bring the water to a gentle boil.
  5. Add the drained rice and stir gently once.
  6. Cook uncovered over medium heat until most of the water has been absorbed and the rice is nearly tender.
  7. Reduce the heat to very low, cover the pot tightly and steam the rice for about 5–8 minutes.
  8. Turn off the heat and let the rice rest, covered, for another 5 minutes.
  9. Gently fluff the rice with a fork and serve.

Tips for Separate, Fluffy Rice

  • Rinse the rice well to remove excess surface starch.
  • Soaking helps the grains cook more evenly.
  • Avoid stirring the rice repeatedly while it cooks.
  • Keep the heat low during the final steaming stage.
  • Let the rice rest before fluffing it.

What to Serve With Basmati Rice

Plain basmati rice is a wonderful accompaniment to Pakistani curries, Chicken Korma, Chicken Karahi, Nihari and lentil dishes such as Daal. It can also be served with raita and a fresh salad.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my basmati rice become sticky?

Excess starch, too much water or overcooking can make rice sticky. Rinse the rice thoroughly and avoid using more water than necessary.

Should basmati rice be soaked?

Soaking is not absolutely required, but a short soaking period can help the grains cook more evenly and become longer and fluffier.

How do I prevent rice from breaking?

Handle the rice gently, avoid excessive stirring and allow it to rest before fluffing.
