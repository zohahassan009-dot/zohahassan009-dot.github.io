Pakistani Beef Kofta Curry

Juicy beef meatballs simmered in a rich, aromatic tomato and yogurt gravy. A comforting Pakistani dinner served with naan or steamed basmati rice.

Prep: 20 minutes
Cook: 45 minutes
Servings: 4–5
Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

For the koftas

  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped and squeezed dry
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh coriander
  • 2 tablespoons roasted gram flour
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • Salt to taste

For the curry

  • 2 medium onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt, whisked
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/3 cup cooking oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 cup water, or as needed
  • Fresh coriander and ginger for garnish

Instructions

  1. Combine all kofta ingredients and mix until the mixture holds together. Shape into evenly sized meatballs.
  2. Heat oil in a deep pan and fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and stir for about a minute. Add tomato puree and turmeric, then cook until thickened.
  4. Reduce the heat and stir in the whisked yogurt. Cook the masala until the oil begins to separate.
  5. Pour in the water and bring the gravy to a gentle simmer.
  6. Carefully add the meatballs in a single layer. Cover and cook over low to medium heat for approximately 20–25 minutes. Avoid stirring vigorously so they do not break.
  7. Check that the meatballs are fully cooked to an internal temperature of 71°C (160°F). Add water if the gravy becomes too thick.
  8. Finish with garam masala, fresh coriander and ginger. Serve hot with naan or basmati rice.

Cooking Tips

Squeeze excess moisture from the chopped onion to help the koftas hold their shape. Let the meatballs simmer undisturbed for the first few minutes, then gently move the pan if needed.
