Pakistani Pistachio Kulfi

Pistachio Kulfi is a creamy and refreshing traditional South Asian frozen dessert. Made with slowly reduced milk, pistachios and fragrant cardamom, this homemade kulfi is a delicious treat for warm days and special occasions.

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 35–40 minutes
Freezing Time: 6 hours or overnight
Servings: 6

Ingredients

  • 1 liter full-fat milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar, or to taste
  • 1/3 cup pistachios, finely chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon cardamom powder
  • 2 tablespoons milk powder, optional
  • 2 tablespoons cream, optional
  • A few extra pistachios for garnish

How to Make Pistachio Kulfi

  1. Pour the milk into a heavy-bottomed saucepan and bring it to a gentle boil over medium heat.
  2. Reduce the heat and simmer the milk, stirring regularly so it does not stick to the bottom of the pan.
  3. Continue cooking until the milk reduces noticeably and becomes slightly creamy and thicker.
  4. Add the sugar, chopped pistachios and cardamom powder. Stir well.
  5. If using milk powder or cream, add them now and mix until smooth.
  6. Cook for another 5–7 minutes over low heat, then turn off the heat.
  7. Allow the mixture to cool to room temperature.
  8. Pour the mixture into kulfi molds or small freezer-safe containers.
  9. Cover and freeze for at least 6 hours or overnight until completely firm.
  10. Before serving, let the kulfi sit at room temperature for a few minutes. Garnish with chopped pistachios and serve.

Tips for Creamy Kulfi

  • Use full-fat milk for a richer texture.
  • Cook the milk slowly for better flavor and creaminess.
  • Stir regularly while reducing the milk to prevent burning.
  • Do not fill the molds completely to leave room for expansion.
  • Freeze the kulfi until completely firm before unmolding.

How to Serve Kulfi

Serve pistachio kulfi chilled and garnish it with chopped pistachios. A small sprinkle of cardamom or a few saffron strands can also add extra aroma and visual appeal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make Kulfi without molds?

Yes. Small freezer-safe containers, cups or a loaf-style freezer container can be used instead of traditional kulfi molds.

How long does homemade Kulfi take to freeze?

It usually needs several hours to become completely firm. Freezing it overnight is a convenient option.

Can I use other nuts?

Yes. Almonds or a combination of pistachios and almonds can be used according to your preference.
