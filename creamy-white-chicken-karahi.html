Creamy White Chicken Karahi

Enjoy this rich and creamy Pakistani-style white chicken karahi, made with tender chicken, yogurt, fresh cream and aromatic spices.

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 35 minutes
Total Time: 50 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 750 g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt, whisked
  • 1/2 cup fresh cream
  • 1/4 cup cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 3–4 green chilies, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon crushed black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon roasted cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon crushed coriander seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Fresh ginger and coriander for garnish

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a karahi over medium heat. Add the chicken and fry for 5–7 minutes until lightly golden.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add salt, black pepper, cumin and crushed coriander. Mix well.
  4. Reduce the heat. Gradually add whisked yogurt while stirring to prevent curdling.
  5. Cover and cook on low-medium heat for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is fully cooked.
  6. Add fresh cream and green chilies. Simmer gently for 3–5 minutes until the sauce becomes rich and creamy.
  7. Stir in lemon juice. Garnish with ginger, coriander and extra green chilies.
  8. Serve hot with fresh naan or chapati.

Cooking Tips

Use thick yogurt and keep the heat low when adding cream. Avoid boiling the sauce vigorously to maintain its smooth texture. Adjust the green chilies according to your preferred spice level.

Serving Suggestions

Serve creamy white chicken karahi with garlic naan, tandoori roti or steamed basmati rice.
