Authentic Pakistani Chicken Pulao

Chicken Pulao is a comforting and aromatic Pakistani rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, tender chicken, whole spices and a rich homemade stock. It is a simple yet delicious meal that is perfect for family lunches, dinners and special gatherings.

Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 50 minutes
Servings: 5

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into medium pieces
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cloves
  • 3 green cardamom pods
  • 1 black cardamom
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • 2–3 green chilies
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 5 cups water, approximately
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

How to Make Chicken Pulao

  1. Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for about 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a large heavy-bottomed pot. Add the sliced onion and cook until golden brown.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for about one minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the chicken pieces and cook over medium heat until the chicken changes color and becomes lightly browned.
  5. Add the chopped tomatoes, yogurt, cumin seeds, cinnamon, cloves, cardamom, bay leaf, black peppercorns and green chilies.
  6. Add red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder and salt. Mix everything well and cook until the tomatoes become soft and the oil begins to separate.
  7. Add approximately 5 cups of water and bring the mixture to a gentle boil. Cook until the chicken becomes tender and the stock develops a rich flavor.
  8. Drain the soaked rice and add it to the pot. Stir gently to distribute the rice evenly.
  9. Cook over medium heat until most of the water has been absorbed.
  10. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat for about 15–20 minutes until the rice becomes fluffy and completely cooked.
  11. Turn off the heat and let the pulao rest for a few minutes. Gently fluff the rice with a fork.
  12. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.

Tips for Perfect Chicken Pulao

  • Use good-quality basmati rice for long and fluffy grains.
  • Soaking the rice helps it cook more evenly.
  • Do not stir the rice too much after adding it to the pot.
  • Use a heavy-bottomed pot to prevent the rice from sticking or burning.
  • Allow the pulao to rest for a few minutes before serving.
  • Adjust the spices and green chilies according to your taste.

What to Serve With Chicken Pulao

Chicken Pulao tastes delicious with raita, fresh salad, mint chutney or a simple yogurt sauce. Lemon wedges and fresh coriander can also add a refreshing finishing touch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use boneless chicken?

Yes. Boneless chicken can be used for Chicken Pulao, although bone-in chicken can add more flavor to the stock.

How do I keep the rice fluffy?

Use properly soaked basmati rice, measure the liquid carefully and avoid stirring the rice too much while it cooks.

Can I make Chicken Pulao less spicy?

Yes. Reduce the amount of red chili powder and green chilies according to your preferred level of spice.

Why should the pulao rest before serving?

Allowing the pulao to rest for a few minutes helps the rice settle, absorb remaining moisture and become easier to fluff without breaking the grains.
