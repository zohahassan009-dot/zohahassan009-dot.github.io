Pakistani Daal Chawal Recipe

Daal Chawal is a comforting Pakistani-style meal made with flavorful lentils served alongside fragrant basmati rice. It is simple, filling and perfect for an easy homemade lunch or dinner.

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 30–35 minutes
Servings: 3–4 people

Ingredients for Daal

  • 1 cup yellow lentils or split moong dal
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 green chilies, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Ingredients for Chawal

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon cooking oil

How to Make Daal

  1. Wash the lentils thoroughly and soak them for about 15–20 minutes.
  2. Add the lentils, water, turmeric, salt and chopped tomatoes to a pot.
  3. Cook over medium heat until the lentils become soft and tender.
  4. Lightly mash the cooked lentils to create a smooth consistency.
  5. In a separate pan, heat oil or ghee.
  6. Add cumin seeds and chopped onion, then cook until lightly golden.
  7. Add ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder and green chilies.
  8. Cook the tempering for a few minutes and pour it over the prepared daal.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander.

How to Cook Basmati Rice

  1. Wash the basmati rice until the water becomes relatively clear.
  2. Soak the rice for about 15 minutes, then drain.
  3. Bring water, salt and oil to a boil in a pot.
  4. Add the rice and cook until the grains are tender.
  5. Drain any excess water if necessary.
  6. Cover the pot and let the rice rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Helpful Cooking Tips

  • Wash the lentils well before cooking.
  • Adjust the amount of water depending on how thick you prefer your daal.
  • Ghee can be used instead of oil for a richer flavor.
  • Use good-quality basmati rice for fluffy, separate grains.
  • Serve the daal and rice while warm for the best experience.

How to Serve Daal Chawal

Daal Chawal can be served with fresh salad, lemon wedges, green chutney or plain yogurt. A little pickle on the side also pairs wonderfully with this comforting meal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Daal Chawal?

Daal Chawal is a popular South Asian-style combination of cooked lentils and rice. It is a simple and comforting homemade meal.

Which daal is best for Daal Chawal?

Yellow moong dal, masoor dal or a combination of different lentils can be used, depending on your preferred taste and texture.

Can I prepare Daal Chawal ahead of time?

Yes. Cooked daal can be refrigerated and reheated. Rice is best enjoyed fresh, but it can also be stored safely and reheated properly.
