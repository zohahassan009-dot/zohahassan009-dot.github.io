Lahori Chicken

A flavorful Pakistani-style chicken curry inspired by the rich and spicy flavors of Lahore.

About Lahori Chicken

Lahori Chicken is a delicious Pakistani-style chicken dish made with tender chicken, tomatoes, yogurt and aromatic spices. It has a rich, flavorful gravy and is perfect for a family lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 medium onion, finely sliced
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 green chilies, sliced
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

How to Make Lahori Chicken

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the sliced onion until lightly golden.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for about one minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken and cook on medium heat until it changes color and becomes lightly golden.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, cumin powder and salt.
  5. Cover and cook until the tomatoes become soft and the chicken is tender.
  6. Whisk the yogurt and gradually add it to the chicken while stirring continuously.
  7. Cook until the gravy becomes rich and the oil begins to separate.
  8. Add green chilies and garam masala. Cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.

Serving Suggestion

Serve Lahori Chicken with naan, roti or fragrant basmati rice. A fresh salad and raita make it even more delicious.

Cooking Tips

  • Use fresh tomatoes for a naturally rich gravy.
  • Cook the chicken well before adding the yogurt.
  • Add yogurt gradually to help prevent curdling.
  • Adjust the spices according to your taste.
